How it works, no jargon

Five steps, in the order Wikipedia accepts

Think of a notary process: first the documents, then the record, finally a request for an outsider to stamp it.

1

Gather the proof AI + you

Like sorting documents for a notary: newspaper articles, interviews, awards. The AI searches and opens each page; you remember where things appeared and send what you have.

2

Create the account with your name you

Like signing a document: a person's name, not a brand. Password and CAPTCHA are always yours.

3

Declare the connection AI writes, you approve

Like saying "I have a stake in this" before speaking in a meeting. It is the account's first edit.

4

Fill in the Wikidata record AI

Like a form where every field has its receipt stapled on: "founded the company", with the link to the article that says so.

5

Draft and review request AI writes, you send

The article sits on your own draft page. An independent editor reads it, adjusts it and decides whether it goes live.

The precautions that prevent a deleted article

🚫 Do not write like an ad

No "renowned", "leading", "benchmark". Only facts, each with a source.

🚫 Do not use a brand name for the account

Accounts with a company name are refused. It happened in the real case and was fixed.

🚫 Do not ask friends to create it

Several accounts, or friends who do not declare the connection, get detected and can get everyone blocked.

🚫 Do not publish directly

With a connection, the text goes to a draft and an outsider reviews it.

🚫 Do not use paid sources as proof

An article marked as "advertorial" only confirms simple facts, like a date.

🚫 Do not fight over the text afterwards

Once published, the article is not yours. Ask for changes on the talk page.

What this guide does not promise

It does not guarantee a Wikipedia article: the community of editors decides. It does make sure you take the right path, with your best material, and know honestly what is still missing.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The AI does the research and writes. You read, approve, enter the password and solve the CAPTCHAs.

Can I add my new company?

On Wikidata, maybe, if there is a public source. On Wikipedia, only once independent newspapers or magazines have written about it. The guide says so honestly.

How long does it take?

Wikidata: a few hours, if the articles exist. Wikipedia article: it depends on the editors' review, from days to weeks.

What if I already have an article?

Do not edit the article about you. Propose changes on its talk page, with a source, and declare your connection.

Can I pay someone to do it?

Paid editing has its own rules at Wikimedia and must be declared. Doing it yourself, with the connection declared, is simpler and more transparent.

Does this really show up on Google and in AIs?

Wikidata and Wikipedia are open and widely used by search engines and by those who train AIs. There is no guarantee of when or how each service will use them. The sure gain is having a neutral, sourced record linked to your website.
